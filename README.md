# CART-470-Personal-Project-Journal
This is the project journal for CART 470.

### Journal Entry Week 2

&emsp;Our project - an interactive soundscape to explore the Concordia campus - has begun relatively smoothly. Since we have not yet met with our client, Florian Grond, the vision for what the final product will look like is a little vague and varies slightly between each of my team members. For example, the project brief describes select points of interest around the Concordia campus wherein the soundscape experience would occur, but some group members believed travelling between points of interest should happen within a 3D space, whereas others (me included) believed these locations should be navigated via the campus map. Hopefully, after meeting with Professor Grond, our differing visions will converge, at least temporarily. 

&emsp;Looking into the resources available, as well as the resources given by the great guest speaker, Professor Grond, and some classmates, it has become clear that our project won’t be built from the ground up but rather would be more of a cohesive organization of various assets (topolocal maps, floor plans, immersive audio). We initially saw this project as a grounded, straightforward tour of the school; however, after our presentation, we realized it can be much more creative and abstract than we planned. It is an art project, after all. 

&emsp;Furthermore, we have since realized that aiding the visually impaired should be incorporated into our design.
Regarding the work we have done in preparing to meet the client, our group has gathered some background data about Concordia’s campus, finding a floor map of one of the buildings which includes a 360-degree view of one of the rooms (found at https://www.concordia.ca/hospitality/venues/concordia-conference-centre/main-floor-plan-seating-capacity.html). We also formulated some questions, created a Photoshop mock-up of the basics of the project, and coordinated a Discord server and Fizzy board.
